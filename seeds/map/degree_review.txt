Mechanics of Materials & Machine Element Design
  Classes of materials
  Microstructure of metals
    FCC, BCC, HCP; grain size effects on mechanical, thermal, electrical, magnetic properties
      Hall-Petch: σy = σ0 + k/√d
    Upper and lower yield point, Lüders bands
    Effects of heat treatment
    Phases of steel (ferrite, pearlite, martensite, austenite)
      ! TTT / CCT diagrams
  Properties of plastics from molecular structure

  Defining and calculating stresses
    Mohr's circle (tensile/compressive vs shear)
    Core stress formulas: δ = FL/EA, σ = My/I, τ = Tr/J, τ = VQ/It, φ = TL/JG, σhoop = Pr/t

  Ductile, brittle and fatigue failure
    Lüders bands; yield as a function of grains and defects
    Ductile failure theories: Coulomb-Mohr, von Mises (distortion energy), Tresca (max shear)
    Griffith crack propagation, K_IC, crack types, plastic zone: K = βσ√(πa)
    Endurance limit; modified Goodman, Soderberg, Gerber
  Machine elements
    AGMA equations for gear life and forces
    Bolted joints: fatigue, stiffness, preload, pull-through
    L10 bearing life and forces
    Tabular stress concentration factors, fit classes
    Thermal strains: ε = αΔT
  Finite element analysis
    Hooke's law, shape functions, strain energy density, stiffness/displacement/force matrices
